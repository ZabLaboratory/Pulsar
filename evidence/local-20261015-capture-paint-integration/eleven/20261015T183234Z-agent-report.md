## Limits and continuation

This qualifies the rebuilt plugin with the recorded dependency runtime, not a
complete b0b62cd source/binary distribution. No package was published or admitted
to the shared Prism development runtime. Physical capture/driver availability,
exhaustive blend/framing coverage and long-duration resource behavior remain
separate qualifications.

Arbitrary alpha/luminance masks and composited group masks remain open. Current
analytic ancestor rectangles cannot represent their coverage, and applying a
group mask independently to overlapping children changes the resulting pixels.
A real group-composition/coverage contract is required before that claim.

Retain this integration worktree and the historical source worktree. The build
cache is intentionally untracked and is not staged. No foreign edits, worktrees,
credentials or shared runtime pins were changed.
